The
Papacy

From a fisherman of Galilee to the Bishop of Rome, an unbroken line of successors has guarded the faith for two thousand years.

The Pope is the Bishop of Rome and the visible head of the Catholic Church. The crossed keys of gold and silver recall the keys of the Kingdom entrusted by Christ to Peter.

    History
    of the
    Church

    Twenty centuries of saints, councils and storms, carried by one promise: “I am with you always.”

    c.33JERUSALEM

    Pentecost

    The Holy Spirit descends on the Apostles gathered with Mary. Peter preaches, and the Church is born.

    313MILAN

    The Edict of Milan

    Emperor Constantine grants Christians freedom of worship; the age of the catacombs ends.

    325NICAEA

    The First Ecumenical Council

    The bishops profess that the Son is “one in being with the Father”, the heart of the Nicene Creed.

    529MONTE CASSINO

    St Benedict and the monasteries

    “Ora et labora”: monasteries keep learning, prayer and farming alive across a fractured Europe.

    1054CONSTANTINOPLE

    The East–West Schism

    Rome and Constantinople break communion, dividing Latin and Greek Christianity.

    c.1210ASSISI & TOULOUSE

    The Mendicant Friars

    St Francis and St Dominic found orders of poverty and preaching that renew the Church from within.

    1517WITTENBERG

    The Reformation

    Martin Luther’s theses open a division that splits Western Christendom.

    1545 – 1563TRENT

    The Council of Trent

    The Catholic Reformation clarifies doctrine, reforms the clergy and inspires the Baroque.

    1869 – 1870ROME

    The First Vatican Council

    Defines papal infallibility before being suspended by the capture of Rome.

    1962 – 1965ROME

    The Second Vatican Council

    Renews the liturgy and opens the Church to dialogue with the modern world.

    2025ROME

    Jubilee of Hope

    A Holy Year of pilgrimage; in May the conclave elects Leo XIV, the first pope from the United States.
